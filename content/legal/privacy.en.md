# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 6, 2026**

This policy explains, in plain words, what data NewFarmer keeps about you, what it is used for, who it is shared with and what your rights are. It applies to everyone who uses the site, whether in Brazil, the European Union, the United States or anywhere else.

## 1. Who looks after your data

The party responsible for your data (the "controller") is **Matheus Lemes**, based in Jundiaí, São Paulo, Brazil.

For any privacy matter, contact the data protection officer (DPO) at [contact@newfarmer.app](mailto:contact@newfarmer.app).

## 2. What data we keep

We keep only what the site needs to work. We do not ask for ID documents, phone number or farm address. Your card number does not pass through us: Stripe receives it (see section 4).

### Your account

- name, username and e-mail address;
- password: we never store the password itself, only a scrambled version (a bcrypt hash) that cannot be reversed;
- the language and theme (light or dark) you chose;
- the date the account was created and the date you confirmed your e-mail;
- whether the account belongs to the site owner;
- whether you agreed to receive news e-mails, and the code in the unsubscribe link.

### Your subscription

- the customer and subscription identifiers at Stripe, the status, the currency, the amount and the dates of the subscription;
- if you cancel from your Profile: the reason you chose, the text you wrote (if any) and the date of the request. This helps us improve NewFarmer and is deleted together with the account.

Your card number **never** reaches NewFarmer and is not stored by us.

### Your farm and your region

- the city you chose at sign-up or in MyFarm: the city name, its coordinates (latitude and longitude) and the country code;
- the climate type and hemisphere we work out from that city;
- the animals you keep and how many of each;
- the crops you chose;
- the diary: date, type of entry, the animal's name (if you add one) and the text you write.

### Technical and security data

- **sessions:** when you sign in, we create a session to keep you signed in. We store a scrambled version of the session code, when it expires and when you last used the site. A session ends after 1 hour without use and lasts at most 30 days;
- **sign-in attempts:** the username typed (only if it has a valid format), the time and whether it worked. We use this to lock the account for a while after 5 wrong passwords in a row;
- **attempt limits:** counters tied to the IP address or the account, to stop abuse (such as someone trying to guess passwords);
- **activity log:** who did what, on what, the IP address and when, for account actions (for example: signing in, signing out, changing the password, editing the farm or the diary). This log **never** stores passwords, access codes or the text of your diary;
- **error log:** when a page of the site fails, we store the page address (without what comes after the "?"), a short error message (up to 300 characters) and the time. We **never** store the content of the request or its headers. We use this for the security and maintenance of the site. The message may, by chance, contain something you typed;
- **e-mail codes:** the codes in the links to reset your password and to confirm your e-mail, stored scrambled;
- **page measurement:** only if you click "Accept all" on the cookie notice. We store a random visitor identifier, your account (if signed in), the page visited without what comes after the "?", the referring site (only the site address), the date and the approximate city and country you visited from (Vercel works them out from the IP address). **We do not store the IP address** in this measurement;
- **e-mail history:** which news e-mails we sent you and when;
- **contact form:** the name, e-mail and message you write on the Contact page. It goes by e-mail (through Resend) to our contact inbox, only to reply to you, and **is not stored on the site**.

### Monthly calendar by e-mail (no account)

If you ask, on the **What to plant** page, to get the calendar every month, we keep the e-mail, the chosen country, the page language, the date of the request, the date you confirmed, the last month sent and the date you unsubscribed (if you did), plus a random code for the confirm and unsubscribe links. This e-mail is not linked to any account.

### Weather forecasts

We keep weather forecasts for up to 7 days for each place, identified by coordinates rounded to 2 decimal places. This is not linked to any person.

## 3. Why we use your data and on what legal basis

- **To provide the service** (your account, your farm, the diary, guidance for your region and the weather forecast): this is the performance of the contract you accept when you create your account (LGPD art. 7, V; GDPR art. 6(1)(b)).
- **For security and fraud prevention** (sessions, locking after wrong passwords, attempt limits, the activity log and the error log): this is our legitimate interest in protecting you and the site (LGPD art. 7, IX; GDPR art. 6(1)(f)).
- **To send account e-mails** (confirming your e-mail, resetting your password, reminding you of your username): performance of the contract.
- **To bill the subscription** (creating and keeping the subscription, knowing whether it is active): performance of the contract (LGPD art. 7, V; GDPR art. 6(1)(b)).
- **To measure the pages visited** and understand what the site needs to improve: your consent, given by "Accept all" (LGPD art. 7, I; GDPR art. 6(1)(a); and the ePrivacy cookie rule). You can withdraw consent at any time.
- **To send the monthly calendar by e-mail:** your consent, given by ticking the box and confirming through the e-mail we send (LGPD, art. 7, I; GDPR, art. 6(1)(a)). Every e-mail has a one-click link to leave.
- **To send news e-mails (marketing):** only if you agreed, and the basis is consent. Every e-mail has a one-click link to leave the list, and you can also change this under **Profile**.

We do not sell your data. We do not use your data for advertising.

## 4. Who we share data with and where it is kept

We use a few providers to run the site. They only process data to provide that service to NewFarmer.

- **Supabase** (database): the data is stored in Ireland, in the European Union.
- **Vercel** (site hosting): receives the visits to the site and keeps technical logs with the IP address. It may process data in the European Union or the United States, under standard contractual clauses.
- **Resend** (sending e-mails): receives your e-mail address, name and username to send account e-mails and, if you agreed, news e-mails; and the messages from the contact form.
- **Open-Meteo** (weather forecasts): receives the text you type when searching for a city and the city's coordinates, to return the forecast. It does not receive any of your account data.
- **Stripe** (payments): processes the subscription payment, on its own account and as our processor. It receives your e-mail address and card details, which you type in Stripe's own page. Stripe keeps billing data for as long as tax law requires, even after your NewFarmer account is deleted.

### Who has access inside NewFarmer

The site owner (administrator) sees your name, e-mail, username, city, country, animals and crops, creation date, subscription and pages visited. This is for support, billing and news e-mails. The administrator **does not see your diary**. Every time the administrator opens a person's details, this is logged.

We may also have to hand over data when a law or an order from an authority requires it.

## 5. Transfers to other countries

NewFarmer is run from Brazil, the database is in the European Union and some providers may process data in the United States. So your data may be processed in Brazil, the European Union and the United States. In these cases we rely on the safeguards offered by the providers, such as standard contractual clauses.

## 6. How long we keep data

- **Account and farm data:** for as long as the account exists. When the account is deleted, this data is deleted too.
- **Activity log:** 180 days. **Sign-in attempts:** 30 days. These records are not deleted together with the account: they follow their own periods and are deleted after that.
- **Error log:** 180 days.
- **Page measurement:** 180 days.
- **News e-mail history:** for as long as the account exists.
- **Monthly calendar by e-mail:** when you unsubscribe, the e-mail stays stored and marked as unsubscribed (so we stop sending and you can come back whenever you want). To delete it for good, ask through the Contact page. Requests never confirmed are deleted after 30 days.
- **Billing data at Stripe:** for as long as tax law requires.
- **Attempt limits:** about 1 day.
- **Weather forecasts:** up to 7 days.
- **Sessions:** until you sign out, after 1 hour without use, or at most 30 days.
- **Password reset code:** valid for 1 hour. **E-mail confirmation code:** valid for 7 days.

## 7. Your rights

To use any of your rights, send an e-mail to [contact@newfarmer.app](mailto:contact@newfarmer.app) from your account's e-mail address (or tell us your username). We may ask you to confirm that the account is yours. We reply within **15 days** (LGPD), **30 days** (GDPR) or **45 days** (US laws).

Two things you can do yourself on the site, under **Profile**, without asking: **"Download my data"** gives you a file (JSON) with your account, animals, crops, diary, your activity log, your subscription, the pages visited and your marketing e-mail preference; **"Delete my account"** cancels the subscription and deletes the account and its data right away, including the pages visited and the e-mail history, after you type your password.

### In Brazil (LGPD)

You can ask for: confirmation that we process your data, access to the data, correction, anonymization, blocking or deletion of unnecessary data, portability, deletion, information about who we share data with, and withdrawal of consent where consent is the basis for processing. You can also complain to the **ANPD** (Brazil's National Data Protection Authority).

### In the European Union (GDPR)

You can ask for: access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, portability, and you can object to processing based on legitimate interest. You can also complain to the data protection authority in your country (for example, the **CNPD** in Portugal).

### In the United States (CCPA/CPRA and similar state laws)

You can ask to: know what data we have and how we use it, delete it and correct it. You will not be treated differently for using these rights.

NewFarmer **does not sell or share** personal data for cross-context behavioral advertising and does not use sensitive data to infer characteristics about you. That is why the site has no "Do Not Sell or Share" link.

## 8. Cookies

We use 4 essential cookies, which the site needs to work and which do not require consent:

- **nf_session:** keeps you signed in.
- **nf_locale:** remembers your language.
- **nf_theme:** remembers the light or dark theme.
- **nf_consent:** remembers your choice on the cookie notice.

We use 1 cookie only with your consent, when you click "Accept all":

- **nf_vid:** a random visitor identifier, for 1 year, to measure the pages visited (see section 2).

We do not use advertising cookies. To change your choice, use the **Cookie preferences** link in the site footer and on your Profile. `nf_cookie_notice` was the cookie of the old notice: it is now only read, counts as "essential only" and is no longer created. If you click "Essential only", `nf_vid` is not created.

## 9. Children

NewFarmer is not for children under 13. If we find out that an account belongs to a child under 13, it will be deleted.

## 10. Security

We store passwords and access codes only in scrambled form, use a secure connection (HTTPS), block repeated attempts to guess passwords and keep no more data than we need. No system is completely secure; if an incident happens that could put you at risk, we will tell you and the authorities, as the law requires.

The date at the top shows the last update. Important changes will be announced on the site.

**Offline mode:** so the site can open without internet, your device stores the app pages you visited and the notes made offline until they are sent. This stays only on your device and is erased when you click Sign out and when you delete the account.
